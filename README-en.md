## 📍 Free

- **Instant teleport** to any point on the map, with address search (Nominatim/OpenStreetMap) or manual coordinates.
- **Trip simulation** along real streets and roads (OSRM): walking, cycling or driving, at any speed you like.
- **Floating joystick** to move in real time.
- **Session history** with date, duration and place.
- **Auto-stop:** turns off by itself at the time you choose.
- **Export GPX routes** and share favorites as CSV.
- **Up to 5 favorites**, with folders and colors.
- Home screen **widget** and dark mode.
- **No limits** on sessions or time.

## ⚠️ Requirements

- Android 7.0 or later
- The app is available in Spanish and English
- Enable **"Mock locations"** in Developer options
- Select **Fake GPS Nell** as the mock location app

### How to enable mock locations

1. Go to **Settings > About phone**
2. Tap **Build number** 7 times to enable developer options
3. Go to **Settings > Developer options**
4. Tap **Select mock location app**
5. Choose **Fake GPS Nell**

The app has a built-in wizard that walks you through these steps.
